
# 📱 UNINSTALL GUIDE - Parental Monitor App

## Complete Step-by-Step Instructions to Uninstall the App

---

## 🤔 Why Uninstall?

If you've decided to remove the Parental Monitor app from your device, this guide will help you completely uninstall it in just a few simple steps.

---

## ✅ Steps to Uninstall

### **For Android Devices:**

#### **Method 1: Settings App (Recommended)**

1. **Open Settings** 
   - Tap the ⚙️ Settings icon on your home screen or app drawer

2. **Navigate to Apps**
   - Scroll down and tap "Apps" or "Application Manager"
   - (The exact name varies by manufacturer: Samsung uses "Apps", Google uses "App", others may say "Application Manager")

3. **Find Parental Monitor**
   - Scroll through the list and find "Parental Monitor"
   - Tap on it

4. **Tap Uninstall**
   - Tap the "Uninstall" button
   - A confirmation dialog will appear

5. **Confirm Uninstall**
   - Tap "OK" or "Yes" to confirm
   - The app will be removed from your device

---

#### **Method 2: Direct from Home Screen**

1. **Long-press the App**
   - On your home screen, long-press the Parental Monitor app icon
   - A menu will appear

2. **Select Uninstall**
   - Tap "Uninstall" or "Remove"

3. **Confirm**
   - Tap "OK" in the confirmation dialog

---

#### **Method 3: Through Google Play Store**

1. **Open Google Play Store**
   - Tap the Play Store app on your device

2. **Search for "Parental Monitor"**
   - Use the search bar at the top

3. **Tap the App**
   - Tap on "Parental Monitor" in the search results

4. **Tap Uninstall**
   - Press the "Uninstall" button

5. **Confirm Removal**
   - Tap "Uninstall" in the confirmation dialog

---

### **For iPhone/iOS Devices:**

#### **Method 1: Settings App**

1. **Open Settings**
   - Tap the Settings app (⚙️)

2. **Go to General**
   - Scroll down and tap "General"

3. **Select iPhone Storage**
   - Tap "iPhone Storage"

4. **Find Parental Monitor**
   - Scroll down the list and tap "Parental Monitor"

5. **Tap Offload or Delete App**
   - Tap "Offload App" (to keep data) or "Delete App" (to remove all data)
   - Tap "Offload App" or "Delete App" again to confirm

---

#### **Method 2: Home Screen Method**

1. **Long-press the App**
   - On your home screen, long-press the Parental Monitor app icon
   - A menu will appear

2. **Select Remove App**
   - Tap "Remove App"

3. **Choose Delete**
   - Tap "Delete App"

4. **Confirm**
   - Tap "Delete" in the confirmation dialog

---

#### **Method 3: Through App Store**

1. **Open the App Store**
   - Tap the App Store app

2. **Go to Updates**
   - Tap the profile icon in the top-right corner
   - Tap your name

3. **View All Apps**
   - Scroll down and find "Parental Monitor"

4. **Tap the App**
   - Tap on "Parental Monitor"

5. **Uninstall**
   - Tap the cloud icon or "-" icon
   - Tap "Remove" to confirm

---

## ⚠️ Important Information Before Uninstalling

### **Data Deletion:**
- If you used the "Delete All Data" option in the app **before** uninstalling, all your data will have been permanently removed from the server
- The app will no longer monitor your device
- Your parent can still see the app was uninstalled through the parent dashboard

### **Account & Sync:**
- Uninstalling the app will **not** affect your account or other devices
- Other devices with the monitoring app will continue to work normally

### **Reinstallation:**
- If you want to reinstall the app later, you can download it again from the Google Play Store or App Store
- You'll need to set up the app again and sync with your parent's account

### **Parent Notification:**
- Your parent will receive a notification that the app has been uninstalled or removed
- They can monitor the status from their parent dashboard

## ❌ Troubleshooting: Can't Uninstall?

### **If You See "System App - Cannot Uninstall":**

This may appear if the app is set as a device administrator. To fix this:

1. **Remove Device Admin Permissions:**
   - Open Settings
   - Go to "Security" or "Biometric and Security"
   - Tap "Device Admin Apps" or "Administrator Apps"
   - Find "Parental Monitor" and tap it
   - Tap "Deactivate" or "Remove"

2. **Try Uninstalling Again:**
   - Now follow the standard uninstall steps above

---

### **If Uninstall Button Is Grayed Out:**

1. **Restart Your Device:**
   - Turn your phone off and on again
   - Try uninstalling after restart

2. **Check Storage Space:**
   - Ensure you have at least 100MB of free storage
   - Delete some files/apps if needed

3. **Contact Your Parent:**
   - If you still can't uninstall, ask your parent to remove device admin permissions from the parent app

---

### **If the App Keeps Reappearing:**

1. **Disable Background Activity:**
   - Go to Settings → Apps → Parental Monitor
   - Turn off "Allow background activity"
   - Disable notifications

2. **Clear App Cache:**
   - Settings → Apps → Parental Monitor → Storage
   - Tap "Clear Cache" and "Clear Data"

3. **Try Uninstall Again:**
   - Follow the uninstall steps again
